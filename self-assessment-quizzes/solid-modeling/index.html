Conventions

Unless stated otherwise, topology counts refer to a closed, connected, orientable 2-manifold boundary. A face can have an outer loop and inner loops. For multiple shells and loops, use V − E + 2F − L = 2(S − G), where S is the number of connected boundary shells and G is the sum of their genera. A numerical Euler check is necessary, but model validity also requires incidence and geometry checks.
