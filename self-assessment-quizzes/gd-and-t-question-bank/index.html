Before you begin

Instructions and conventions

Use ASME Y14.5-2018 (R2024) conventions unless a question states a simplifying assumption. All dimensions are in millimetres. Basic dimensions are theoretically exact. Unless stated otherwise, a quoted measured feature size is its actual mating size and all local sizes satisfy their limits. For coordinate-based position calculations, assume the stated offsets describe a straight axis parallel to its nominal direction unless tilt is specified. Show the principal steps for every numerical decision and state pass or fail. The boxed feature-control frames are teaching schematics: characteristic names replace geometric symbols. A circled M after a tolerance denotes MMC; after a datum letter it denotes MMB.

  • A diametrical position error based on coordinate offsets is

    \[T_{\mathrm{pos}}=2\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2+(\Delta y)^2}.\]

  • Internal feature at MMC: bonus \(=\) actual mating size \(-\) MMC size.

  • External feature at MMC: bonus \(=\) MMC size \(-\) actual mating size.

  • Unless specifically stated otherwise, ignore measurement uncertainty, surface texture, elastic deformation, and thermal expansion.

Allow about 25–30 minutes per question. Each question is worth 10 marks. This bank contains questions only; use the concepts quiz for immediate feedback.
