Conventions

Use ASME Y14.5 style interpretations. Dimensions are in millimetres. ⌀ means a diametrical tolerance zone; Ⓜ means maximum material condition (MMC). Basic dimensions are theoretically exact. Numerical examples assume idealized features and no measurement uncertainty.

Review and go further

Review the GD&T lesson and interactive lab, then work through the 30 GD&T questions. The references page links to the ASME Y14.5 standard record. This teaching quiz simplifies inspection and does not replace a complete drawing specification.
